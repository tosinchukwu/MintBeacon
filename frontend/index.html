    Honest limits - read before trusting a verdict:
    • LIKELY_FREE means "probably free - deep-check recommended", never a guarantee. The probe saw no payment, but could not prove the mint costs nothing (usually it was stopped by an identity check, or a mint function looked like it reached a state change). A paid mint whose payment check runs after its identity gate can look LIKELY_FREE.
    • FREE means the probe found positive proof the mint costs nothing for the checker (a free-lane getter answering yes, or a price of 0). PAID means a payment error or a price above 0 was seen. UNKNOWN means the probe could not decide - the mint may be closed, sold out, signature-gated, or use a pattern this probe does not recognise.
    • The free probe uses the deployed MintBeacon contract on-chain. If that probe is unavailable, the backend may fall back to the off-chain classifier; a fully open mint can read UNKNOWN in that fallback because a plain eth_call simulates the mint and discards it, while the on-chain probe catches the state-change attempt.
    • The probe never mints from the target, never sends it value, and never looks up a wallet by Agent ID name. The only identity question ever asked is "does this address hold an Agent ID?"
    • No promised returns. A "free" verdict says nothing about whether the token will ever sell or be worth anything.
